# STOP spaces restored to event cards

The 32-space board now has 27 question spaces and 5 STOP event cards. STOP spaces 5, 10, 20, 25, and 32 do not ask a quiz question, award correct-answer Life points, or impose an answer penalty.

The original normal completion choices, four failure scenarios, bonus costs ($50/$150/$250/$250), return-to-Start alternatives and success pop-ups remain. Select a named failure scenario on its STOP card to see the bonus and restart choices. Welcome Letter remains a final celebration with no failure/bonus option. Queued movement resumes after the card or bonus success is completed. Random one-on-one PATIENT SEARCH CHALLENGES remain separate.

Upload content.mjs, engine.mjs, test.mjs, public/client.js, public/index.html and public/style.css to the matching GitHub paths and redeploy Render. Refresh all player devices afterward. This package does not automatically update the published site.
