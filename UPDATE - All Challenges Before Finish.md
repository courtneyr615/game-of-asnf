# All patient challenge questions are asked before the end

The six unique challenge questions are shared across the whole room, not required individually for every player. Random triggers remain, with progress-based catch-up triggers to prevent unused questions being missed. If any remain when the patients reach the lodge, the game opens final one-on-one challenge rounds and withholds final results until those questions have winners.

Each round still involves only the challenger and one selected opponent. First correct server-received answer wins $500 once. The deck never repeats questions. The challenger continues after each result; any final rounds then lead to the final scores. Cash won in these final rounds counts toward the winner.
