# Prominent failures and optional sound

Failure wording now appears in bold inside a red border with a light red background. Incorrect-answer feedback uses the same red treatment.

Sound effects are optional and begin off. Use Sound: Off / Sound: On in the header or pop-up windows to toggle them. Sounds cover spinning, new cards, correct/incorrect answers, and bonus/challenge wins. They are synthesized in the browser and use no downloaded music. The setting is saved on that device.

Upload public/audio.js, public/client.js, public/index.html and public/style.css, or upload the full package, then redeploy Render and refresh every device. Keep the persistent disk. The downloadable package does not update the live website by itself.
