# PATIENT SEARCH CHALLENGE update

Six supplied multiple-choice questions are now included. Ordinary Life-card landings have a 25% chance to trigger one. The current player selects one opponent; only the two selected players see the question. First correct server-received answer wins $500 in game money. The server awards the prize once, even for simultaneous submissions. Wrong challenge answers cost 1 Life point, or $50 if no Life points remain, and allow another try. Other players continue seeing the shared board while waiting. The challenger dismisses the result and completes the regular landing question; queued movement remains intact.

On one-screen play, answer groups are labeled by player. That mode uses the first correct click on the shared device; use the hosted multiplayer version for separate-device races.

The six cases are fictional training examples. The two prescreen answers are specific to their supplied case, not general insurance or income eligibility policy.

Upload content.mjs, engine.mjs, test.mjs, public/client.js, public/index.html and public/style.css to the matching GitHub paths and let Render deploy. Have all players refresh after deployment. Existing saved games and balances remain on the persistent disk. This package has not been deployed automatically.
